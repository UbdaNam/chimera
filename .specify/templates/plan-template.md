## Constitution Check

_GATE: Must pass before Phase 0 research. Re-check after Phase 1 design._

All plans submitted for execution MUST include a Constitution Check section demonstrating compliance with the Project Chimera Constitution. At minimum the plan MUST assert and provide evidence for the following gates:

- Spec-Driven: Link to the ratified specification that authorizes the work.
- Hierarchical Swarm Compliance: Describe agent roles, hierarchy, and escalation paths used by the plan.
- Super-Orchestrator Approval: Indicate Super-Orchestrator identity and approval status for high-risk operations or management-by-exception cases.
- MCP Usage: Confirm all external interactions will be mediated through MCP interfaces.
- Phase Separation: Confirm that planning outputs exist and that no implementation will begin before ratification.
- Auditability: Provide artifact identifiers and storage locations for immutable specifications and audit logs.

Plans that do not satisfy these gates MUST be rejected or returned for revision.
