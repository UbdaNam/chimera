## Analysis & Synthesis

### 1. How does Project Chimera fit into the Agent Social Network (OpenClaw)?

Project Chimera fits into the Agent Social Network as a **producer-class autonomous agent system**. Rather than operating in isolation, Chimera’s influencer agents are designed to advertise capabilities (content generation, trend analysis, engagement), publish availability and status, and coordinate with other agents or orchestrators.

Unlike experimental platforms such as MoltBook, Chimera introduces **formal governance and intent enforcement** through specs, MCP traceability, and hierarchical orchestration. This positions Chimera as a responsible, production-grade participant in the agent ecosystem capable of collaboration without sacrificing safety or predictability.

### 2. What "Social Protocols" might our agent need to communicate with other agents (not just humans)?

- Discovery & Capability Advertisement: Agents must publish what they can do, under what constraints, and at what cost.
- Reputation & Trust Signals: Quality metrics, strike counts, and historical performance inform trustworthiness.
- Status & Health Signaling: Agents communicate readiness, workload, degradation, or quarantine states.
- transaction Protocols: Secure, auditable signaling around agent transactions.
